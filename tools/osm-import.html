Import places from OpenStreetMap

A build tool, not part of Babnav. It asks OpenStreetMap what is mapped inside a box around campus and turns the answer into js/data.js entries.

Check what comes back. OpenStreetMap is edited by volunteers. A building may be misnamed, out of date, or drawn in the wrong spot. Treat this as a fast first draft, then verify the ones that matter before setting verified: true.
